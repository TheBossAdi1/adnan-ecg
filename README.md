# ∿ Adnan ECG

**A free 12-lead ECG simulator for medical students.**
Pick a rhythm, watch a realistic ECG appear, give drugs and see how the trace changes, then test yourself with the quiz.

### 

No sign-up. No payment. No installation. It runs right in your browser, on your phone or computer.

---

## What you can do

- **Study ECG patterns.** Browse a library of rhythms and conditions, from normal sinus rhythm to atrial fibrillation, AV blocks, ventricular tachycardia, infarction, hypertrophy and electrolyte disturbances. Use the search box to find one fast.
- **Watch drugs work over time.** Give a drug and see the ECG change as it takes effect. Slow drugs like digoxin and amiodarone can be sped up so you don't have to wait.
- **Try interventions.** Defibrillate, cardiovert or use a vagal manoeuvre and see what happens.
- **See it two ways.** View the full 12-lead ECG on millimetre paper, the monitor strip, or both at once. Change the paper speed (25 or 50 mm/s) and the gain (5, 10 or 20 mm/mV), or freeze the trace.
- **Read the numbers live.** Heart rate, axis, QT, and ST elevation or depression in each lead, plus effective potassium and calcium.
- **Learn why it looks that way.** Each pattern comes with its ECG criteria, the mechanism behind it, and a short clinical memo. The Waves and Leads tabs explain every wave, interval and lead.
- **Take the quiz.** Get a random ECG to interpret, with instant feedback, a score, your streak and a list of patterns you've already seen.
- **Save your ECG.** Download the current tracing as an image, or double-click it for fullscreen.

## How to use it

1. Choose a pattern from the list on the left, or search for one.
2. Use the tabs on the right: **Explanation**, **Waves**, **Leads**, **Drugs** and **Quiz**.
3. In **Drugs**, pick a drug and a dose, press **Administer**, and watch the ECG and the live measurements change.
4. Tap **Restart** to go back to the beginning.

## Languages and themes

The whole app is available in **Italian, English, French, German, Romanian, Arabic and Farsi**. Change the language with the menu at the top right.

You can also switch between **Day**, **Sunset** and **Night** themes.

## Privacy

Everything happens on your device. The app doesn't collect personal data, and there are no accounts. Your quiz progress and language choice are saved only in your own browser.

## ⚠️ Medical disclaimer

Adnan ECG is for **education and practice only**. The ECGs are computer-generated, not real patient recordings. It is **not** a medical device, a diagnostic tool or a prescribing guide, and it must never be used to make decisions about real patients. Drug doses and criteria are based on published guidelines and product information, which always take precedence. Quiz scores are not a measure of professional competence.
